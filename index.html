About Me

Hey! I'm Nikhil Kumar, a Computer Science Engineering student at Chandigarh University with a serious passion for building things — whether it's a full-stack web app, an IoT prototype, or figuring out why my code broke at 2 AM.

I'm drawn to problems that actually matter. Not the kind that live in textbooks, but the kind that people deal with every day. That's what drives me to keep learning, keep building, and keep pushing code until something clicks.

When I'm not behind a screen, you'll find me out on my bike — open roads have a way of clearing the head. I'm also into gaming, and I genuinely love exploring new things, whether it's a new tech stack, a new framework, or a new genre of music on a long ride.

Right now? I'm actively looking for opportunities where I can contribute, grow fast, and work on technology that makes a real dent in real problems.
